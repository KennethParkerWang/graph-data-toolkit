## Connectivity/attack coverage after the follow-up

Implemented additions are edge-removal LCC sums, directed weak/strong LCC sums, actual cumulative-fraction-grid batching and checked random-repeat statistics. These do not exhaust all definitions of network robustness. Further distinct work includes mixed node/edge attacks, localized/group/correlated failures, remaining-population percentage and Bernoulli protocols, recovery, probabilistic all-terminal/two-terminal reliability, dedicated percolation-threshold estimators, and topology cut-ratio measures such as toughness/integrity/scattering. Each needs a separately specified definition, algorithm/source and validation; no alias or uncomputed placeholder is registered.
